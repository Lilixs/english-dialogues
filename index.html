Dialogue 1 · Morning at Home
在家里的早晨

Ben rents a room from Mrs. Chen. It is breakfast time.
本租住在陈阿姨家。现在是早餐时间。

🔁 Repeated: 0 times · 已重复：0 次
  1. Mrs. ChenGood morning, Ben. Did you sleep well?早上好，本。你昨晚睡得好吗？
  2. BenMorning, Mrs. Chen. Yes, I slept very well, thank you.早上好，陈阿姨。睡得很好，谢谢。
  3. Mrs. ChenBreakfast is ready. Eggs and toast today.早餐好了。今天吃鸡蛋和吐司。
  4. BenSmells great. What are your plans for today?闻起来真香。您今天有什么安排？
  5. Mrs. ChenI'm going to the market. And you?我要去市场。你呢？
  6. BenI'm going to the library this morning. Then I'll fix my bike.我上午去图书馆。然后修我的自行车。
  7. Mrs. ChenIt's cold outside. Take your warm jacket.外面冷。穿上你的厚外套。
  8. BenGood idea. Oh no — where are my keys?好主意。哎呀——我的钥匙呢？
  9. Mrs. ChenLook, they're on the table, next to your book.看，在桌子上，你书的旁边。
  10. BenHa! Thank you. See you at dinner.哈！谢谢。晚饭见。

Dialogue 2 · The Neighbour's Garden
邻居的花园

Ben meets his neighbour, Mrs. Ortiz, outside her house.
本在邻居奥尔蒂斯太太家门外遇见她。

🔁 Repeated: 0 times · 已重复：0 次
  1. BenHi, Mrs. Ortiz. Your garden looks beautiful.嗨，奥尔蒂斯太太。您的花园真漂亮。
  2. Mrs. OrtizThank you, Ben. The tomatoes are doing well this year.谢谢，本。今年的西红柿长得很好。
  3. BenCan I help you with anything?有什么需要我帮忙的吗？
  4. Mrs. OrtizActually, yes. We're going away this weekend.还真有。我们这周末要出门。
  5. Mrs. OrtizCould you water my flowers on Saturday and Sunday?你能在周六和周日帮我浇花吗？
  6. BenOf course. Morning or evening?当然。早上还是晚上？
  7. Mrs. OrtizEvening is better. The sun is too strong at noon.晚上好一点。中午太阳太强了。
  8. BenNo problem. I'll water them at seven.没问题。我七点浇。
  9. Mrs. OrtizYou're very kind. I'll bring you some tomatoes when we're back.你真好。我们回来给你带些西红柿。
  10. BenSounds great. Have a good trip!太好了。祝你们旅途愉快！

Dialogue 3 · At the Corner Store
在街角小店

Ben goes to the small shop on his street. Rosa works there.
本去街上的小店。罗莎在那里工作。

🔁 Repeated: 0 times · 已重复：0 次
  1. BenHi, Rosa. How's business today?嗨，罗莎。今天生意怎么样？
  2. RosaHi, Ben. Quiet this morning. What do you need?嗨，本。今天上午很安静。你需要什么？
  3. BenA bottle of milk and six eggs, please.一瓶牛奶和六个鸡蛋。
  4. RosaHere you are. Anything else?给你。还要别的吗？
  5. BenDo you have any fresh bread?有新鲜面包吗？
  6. RosaYes, it came in an hour ago. It's still warm.有，一小时前刚到的。还是热的。
  7. BenGreat, I'll take one. How much is everything?太好了，我要一个。一共多少钱？
  8. RosaThat's eight dollars fifty.一共八块五。
  9. BenHere's ten. Keep the change... no wait, I need the coins for the bus.给你十块。零钱不用找了……等一下，我需要硬币坐公交车。
  10. RosaHa, no worries. Here's your change. Have a good day!哈，没关系。这是找你的钱。祝你今天愉快！

Dialogue 4 · A Phone Call
一通电话

Ben calls his sister Kate about Sunday dinner.
本给姐姐凯特打电话，问周日晚饭的事。

🔁 Repeated: 0 times · 已重复：0 次
  1. KateHello?喂？
  2. BenHi, Kate. It's Ben. What's up?嗨，凯特。我是本。最近怎么样？
  3. KateOh, hi! Not much. I'm just cooking dinner.哦，嗨！没什么。我正在做晚饭。
  4. BenI'm calling about Sunday. Are we still having dinner at your place?我打电话是问周日的事。我们还是在你家吃晚饭吗？
  5. KateYes! Come at six. Mom and Dad will be there too.对！六点来。爸妈也会来。
  6. BenCan I bring anything?我需要带点什么吗？
  7. KateCould you bring a cake? The kids love cake.你能带个蛋糕吗？孩子们喜欢吃蛋糕。
  8. BenSure. Chocolate or lemon?当然。巧克力的还是柠檬的？
  9. KateChocolate, please. And don't be late this time!巧克力的。还有，这次别迟到！
  10. BenHa, I promise. See you on Sunday.哈，我保证。周日见。

Dialogue 5 · Evening in the Park
傍晚的公园

In the evening, Ben meets his friend Ann in the park.
傍晚，本在公园里遇见朋友安。

🔁 Repeated: 0 times · 已重复：0 次
  1. BenHi, Ann. Fancy meeting you here.嗨，安。真巧，在这儿碰到你。
  2. AnnHi, Ben! I come here every evening. I love feeding the ducks.嗨，本！我每天晚上都来。我喜欢喂鸭子。
  3. BenHow was your day?你今天过得怎么样？
  4. AnnBusy, but good. I finished my big project at work.很忙，但是很好。我完成了工作上的大项目。
  5. BenThat's great. You worked on it for weeks.太好了。那个项目你做了好几周。
  6. AnnI know. I feel free now! What about you?是啊。我现在感觉自由了！你呢？
  7. BenI fixed my bike today. It rides like new.我今天修好了自行车。骑起来像新的一样。
  8. AnnNice. Hey, do you want to ride to the lake on Saturday?不错。嘿，你周六想骑车去湖边吗？
  9. BenI'd love to. Let's meet here at ten in the morning.我很想去。我们上午十点在这里见。
  10. AnnPerfect. See you then. Don't forget your camera!太好了。到时见。别忘了带相机！
